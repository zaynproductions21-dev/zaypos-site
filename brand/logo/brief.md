# ZayPOS logo brief

## Brand

- **What it does:** a browser-based till for UK food and drink: order-taking, card payments, stock, staff and reports, with food-safety (EHO) records and AI invoice processing built in. Works offline, runs on hardware the venue already owns. £29 / £69 / £99 per till per month, 14-day free trial, no contract. Built and supported in Gloucester by Zayn Productions Ltd, under the ZaynIT group.
- **For whom (inferred):** the owner-operator of an independent café, bakery or small restaurant, often running the counter themselves, switching from Square or a legacy till. They pay; their part-time staff use it. They judge the brand on a phone, between services.
- **Must own:** *friendly* — easy, warm, quick at the counter ("the POS your team will actually love using"). It must still look solid enough to trust with the takings.
- **Must never suggest:** *a bank or a fintech*. Not a card terminal company, not crypto, not a generic violet SaaS tile.
- **Where it lives, ranked:** browser tab and home-screen icon on the till tablet → nav at ~28–32px on a cream page → receipts printed on a black-only ESC/POS thermal printer (mono is mandatory, and thermal print hates hairlines) → social avatar (circle) → shop-window sticker / menu footer.
- **Name anatomy:** `ZayPOS` = `Zay|POS`, capital Z and P, the seam at `y|P`. Z is the one letter worth owning (and it ties to Zayn / ZaynIT). `y` has the only descender, sitting right at the seam. O is the only round letter. All caps it is 6 letters, so it fits a nav at any size.
- **The mechanism:** a ticket. An order goes in, a receipt comes out, its torn edge is a zigzag. Also the keypad, the folded receipt roll, the coin/plate.
- **Competitors:** Square (a square inside a rounded square, black), SumUp (bold lowercase wordmark, a wedge mark), Clover (four-leaf clover, green), Toast (a toast slice icon, orange), Lightspeed (a flame), Epos Now (wordmark with a green dot), Tevalis. Stay away from: square-in-a-square, flames, slices, leaves, green.
- **Existing equity:** violet `#5B3EF5`, a warm near-black `#1A1208` on cream `#FDFAF6`, Fraunces serif headings, and a white Georgia "Z" on a violet rounded tile. **Keep** the flat violet (as the one accent, no gradient) and the warm ink; **drop** the tile and the default-font Z. The Z itself stays as the idea to own.

## Concepts considered (16 → 5)

1. Violet rounded tile with a white Z (the current mark): **killed**, the app-tile AI default.
2. Card terminal / card with contactless waves: killed, fintech, and waves are an AI tell.
3. Coffee cup with steam: killed, stock café icon, and ZayPOS is also restaurants.
4. Shopping cart / bag: killed, retail e-commerce.
5. Lightning-bolt Z ("fast"): killed, energy-drink cliché.
6. Fork-and-knife Z: killed, restaurant-directory cliché.
7. Square-in-square: killed, too close to Square.
8. Chef's hat: killed, cliché and kitchen-only.
9. Speech-bubble "order": killed, chat app.
10. Price tag with a Z: killed, retail and reads as a label.
11. **Folded Z.** A strip of receipt paper folded twice makes a Z: top and bottom panels in ink, the diagonal fold in violet. **Kept (01).**
12. **Receipt.** A receipt slip with a zigzag torn edge, the Z cut through it. **Kept (02).**
13. **Keypad Z.** A 3×3 till keypad of dots with a Z traced through seven of them in one stroke. **Kept (03).**
14. **Y-tail wordmark.** ZayPOS in Fraunces Black, the `y` descender swung under `POS` like a total line; icon is the Z with that line. **Kept (04).**
15. **Coin Z.** A solid disc (a coin, a plate) with a fat Z cut right through it: made for the circle avatar. **Kept (05).**
16. Keypad key cap with a Z: killed, it is the tile again.
